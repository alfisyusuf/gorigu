Analisis Gaya & VektorForce & Vector Analysis

Sebelum menghitung angka, gambar dulu arah setiap gaya. Ada dua pola yang wajib kamu kenali. Before crunching numbers, draw the direction of each force first. There are two patterns you must recognize.

🔹 Segaris (Kolinear)Collinear

Jika dua gaya bekerja pada satu garis lurus yang sama: If two forces act along the same straight line:

  • Searah → dijumlahkan.Same direction → add.
  • Berlawanan arah → dikurangkan (diselisihkan), arah hasil mengikuti gaya yang lebih besar.Opposite directions → subtract, the resulting direction follows the larger force.

🔹 Tegak Lurus (Perpendicular)Perpendicular

Jika dua gaya saling tegak lurus (membentuk sudut 90°), gabungkan dengan Teorema Pythagoras: If two forces are perpendicular (forming a 90° angle), combine them using the Pythagorean Theorem:

\( F_{total} = \sqrt{F_1^2 + F_2^2} \)

Kunci SuksesKey to Success Muatan sejenis (+ dan +, atau − dan −) selalu tolak-menolak. Muatan berbeda jenis (+ dan −) selalu tarik-menarik. Gambar anak panahnya dulu, baru hitung besarnya. Like charges (+ and +, or − and −) always repel. Opposite charges (+ and −) always attract. Draw the arrows first, then calculate the magnitude.
✓ Contoh Soal Terpandu — Gaya SegarisGuided Example — Collinear Forces

Tiga muatan segaris. Muatan A (\(+2\,\mu\text{C}\)) di kiri, muatan B (\(-1\,\mu\text{C}\)) di tengah, dan muatan C (\(+4\,\mu\text{C}\)) di kanan. Jarak A–B = 3 cm, dan jarak B–C = 3 cm. Tentukan besar dan arah gaya Coulomb total yang dialami muatan B! Three collinear charges. Charge A (\(+2\,\mu\text{C}\)) is on the left, charge B (\(-1\,\mu\text{C}\)) is in the middle, and charge C (\(+4\,\mu\text{C}\)) is on the right. Distance A–B = 3 cm, and B–C = 3 cm. Determine the magnitude and direction of the total Coulomb force on charge B!

1 A bermuatan + dan B bermuatan −, jadi keduanya tarik-menarik: gaya pada B menuju A (ke kiri). A is + and B is −, so they attract: the force on B is towards A (to the left).
\( F_{BA} = 9\times10^9 \dfrac{(2\times10^{-6})(1\times10^{-6})}{(0.03)^2} = 20\ \text{N} \) (ke kiri)(left)
2 C bermuatan + dan B bermuatan −, jadi keduanya juga tarik-menarik: gaya pada B menuju C (ke kanan). C is + and B is −, so they also attract: the force on B is towards C (to the right).
\( F_{BC} = 9\times10^9 \dfrac{(1\times10^{-6})(4\times10^{-6})}{(0.03)^2} = 40\ \text{N} \) (ke kanan)(right)
3 Kedua gaya berlawanan arah dan segaris, jadi diselisihkan: The forces are opposite and collinear, so we subtract them:
\( \Sigma F_B = F_{BC} - F_{BA} = 40 - 20 = 20\ \text{N} \)
Jawaban: gaya total pada B = 20 N, arahnya ke KANAN (mengikuti gaya yang lebih besar, yaitu tarikan dari C). Answer: total force on B = 20 N, direction is to the RIGHT (following the larger force, which is the pull from C).
Cek Cepat — Pola Tegak LurusQuick Check — Perpendicular Pattern Dua gaya 5 N dan 12 N saling tegak lurus? Langsung pakai Pythagoras: \( \sqrt{5^2+12^2} = \sqrt{169} = 13\ \text{N} \). Ingat-ingat pasangan Pythagoras populer: 3–4–5, 6–8–10, dan 5–12–13 — sering muncul di soal ujian! Two perpendicular forces of 5 N and 12 N? Use Pythagoras directly: \( \sqrt{5^2+12^2} = \sqrt{169} = 13\ \text{N} \). Remember popular Pythagorean triples: 3–4–5, 6–8–10, and 5–12–13 — they appear often in exams!

Hukum Gauss & Potensial ListrikGauss's Law & Electric Potential

Fluks itu sederhana: hitung "seberapa banyak garis medan menembus suatu permukaan". Flux is simple: count "how many field lines pierce through a surface".

🕳️ Fakta Penting: Konduktor BeronggaCrucial Fact: Hollow Conductors

Pada bola konduktor bermuatan, semua muatan tersebar merata di permukaan luar. Akibatnya, medan listrik di dalam konduktor (untuk \( r < R \)) selalu nol — berapa pun besar muatannya. Ini poin yang paling sering ditanyakan sebagai soal benar/salah! On a charged hollow conducting sphere, all charges spread evenly on the outer surface. As a result, the electric field inside the conductor (for \( r < R \)) is always zero — regardless of the total charge. This is the most frequently asked true/false question!

Konsep KunciKey Concept Hukum Gauss: \( \Phi = E \cdot A \cdot \cos\theta = \dfrac{Q_{enclosed}}{\varepsilon_0} \). Untuk muatan titik, permukaan Gauss berbentuk bola dengan luas \( A = 4\pi r^2 \), sehingga didapat rumus medan yang sudah kamu kenal: \( E = k \dfrac{Q}{r^2} \), dengan \( k = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 9\times10^9 \). Gauss's Law: \( \Phi = E \cdot A \cdot \cos\theta = \dfrac{Q_{enclosed}}{\varepsilon_0} \). For a point charge, the Gaussian surface is a sphere with area \( A = 4\pi r^2 \), which gives the familiar field formula: \( E = k \dfrac{Q}{r^2} \), where \( k = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 9\times10^9 \).
✓ Contoh Soal Terpandu — Medan ListrikGuided Example — Electric Field

Tentukan kuat medan listrik pada jarak \( r = 0{,}3 \) m dari muatan titik \( Q = 2\times10^{-6} \) C! Determine the electric field strength at a distance \( r = 0.3 \) m from a point charge \( Q = 2\times10^{-6} \) C!

1 Gunakan rumus \( E = \dfrac{kQ}{r^2} \). Use the formula \( E = \dfrac{kQ}{r^2} \).
2 Masukkan angka:Plug in the numbers: \( E = \dfrac{9\times10^9 \times 2\times10^{-6}}{(0.3)^2} = \dfrac{1{,}8\times10^4}{0{,}09} \)
3 Hasil:Result: \( E = 2\times10^5 \) N/C.
Jawaban: E = 2 × 10⁵ N/C. Answer: E = 2 × 10⁵ N/C.
Bonus — Contoh Potensial ListrikBonus — Electric Potential Example Berapa potensial \( V \) pada \( r = 0{,}5 \) m dari muatan \( Q = 3\times10^{-6} \) C? Gunakan \( V = \dfrac{kQ}{r} = \dfrac{9\times10^9 \times 3\times10^{-6}}{0{,}5} = \dfrac{2{,}7\times10^4}{0{,}5} = 5{,}4\times10^4 \) V. Ingat, \( V \) adalah besaran skalar — kalau ada beberapa muatan, potensialnya cukup dijumlahkan langsung, tanpa Pythagoras! What is the potential \( V \) at \( r = 0.5 \) m from a charge \( Q = 3\times10^{-6} \) C? Use \( V = \dfrac{kQ}{r} = \dfrac{9\times10^9 \times 3\times10^{-6}}{0.5} = \dfrac{2.7\times10^4}{0.5} = 5.4\times10^4 \) V. Remember, \( V \) is a scalar quantity — if there are multiple charges, just add the potentials directly, no Pythagoras needed!

KapasitorCapacitors

Kapasitor menyimpan muatan listrik. Tiga hal yang wajib kamu kuasai: definisi, geometri pelat sejajar, dan rangkaian seri/paralel. Capacitors store electric charge. Three things you must master: definition, parallel-plate geometry, and series/parallel circuits.

🔋 Definisi KapasitansiDefinition of Capacitance

\( C = \dfrac{Q}{V} \) — kapasitansi adalah muatan yang tersimpan per satuan tegangan. Satuannya Farad (F), dinamai dari Michael Faraday. Kapasitor di soal biasanya bersatuan µF (mikrofarad). capacitance is the charge stored per unit of voltage. Its unit is the Farad (F), named after Michael Faraday. In problems, capacitors are usually measured in µF (microfarads).

📐 Kapasitor Pelat SejajarParallel-Plate Capacitor

\( C = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r A}{d} \) — kapasitansi makin besar jika luas pelat (A) makin besar, atau jarak antar pelat (d) makin kecil, atau memakai bahan dielektrik. capacitance increases if the plate area (A) gets larger, or the distance between plates (d) gets smaller, or a dielectric material is used.

Intuisi Seri vs ParalelSeries vs Parallel Intuition SeriSeries: \( \dfrac{1}{C_s} = \dfrac{1}{C_1}+\dfrac{1}{C_2}+... \) — hasil akhirnya selalu lebih KECIL dari kapasitor terkecil dalam rangkaian. the final result is always SMALLER than the smallest capacitor in the circuit.
ParalelParallel: \( C_p = C_1+C_2+... \) — hasil akhirnya selalu lebih BESAR dari kapasitor terbesar dalam rangkaian. the final result is always LARGER than the largest capacitor in the circuit.
✓ Contoh Soal Terpandu — Mencari KapasitansiGuided Example — Finding Capacitance

Sebuah kapasitor menyimpan muatan \( 6\times10^{-4} \) C ketika dihubungkan ke baterai 12 V. Hitung kapasitansinya! A capacitor stores a charge of \( 6\times10^{-4} \) C when connected to a 12 V battery. Calculate its capacitance!

1 Diketahui:Given: \( Q = 6\times10^{-4} \) C, \( V = 12 \) V.
2 GunakanUse \( C = \dfrac{Q}{V} \).
3 \( C = \dfrac{6\times10^{-4}}{12} = 5\times10^{-5}\ \text{F} = 50\ \mu\text{F} \)
Jawaban: C = 50 µF. Answer: C = 50 µF.

Part B — Gauss's Law in Action

20 min

1. In your own words, explain why the shape of a Gaussian surface does not matter when applying Gauss's Law.

2. A uniform field E = 400 N/C passes straight through (θ = 0°) a flat surface of area A = 1.5 m². Find the flux Φ.

Φ = E × A = _______ × _______ = _______ N·m²/C

3. A closed surface encloses a charge Qenc = 4.0 × 10⁻⁹ C. Use ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²) to find the total flux.

Φtotal = Qenc / ε₀ = _______ / _______ = _______ N·m²/C

4. (Challenge) A point charge Q = 5.0 × 10⁻⁹ C sits at the centre of a spherical Gaussian surface of radius r = 0.2 m. Follow the steps to find E.

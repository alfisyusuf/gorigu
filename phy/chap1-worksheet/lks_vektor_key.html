Force & Electric Field Vector Analysis

3. Skill Test Solutions

Problem 1 (Collinear Forces): Three collinear charges. Charge A (\(+2 \, \mu\text{C}\)) is on the left, charge B (\(-1 \, \mu\text{C}\)) is in the middle, and charge C (\(+4 \, \mu\text{C}\)) is on the right. The distance from A to B is \(3 \, \text{cm}\), and from B to C is \(3 \, \text{cm}\). Determine the magnitude and direction of the total Coulomb force experienced by charge B!

Step 1: Calculate magnitudes
\( F_{BA} = 9 \times 10^9 \frac{(2 \times 10^{-6})(1 \times 10^{-6})}{(0.03)^2} = \frac{18 \times 10^{-3}}{9 \times 10^{-4}} = 20 \, \text{N} \) (Left)

\( F_{BC} = 9 \times 10^9 \frac{(1 \times 10^{-6})(4 \times 10^{-6})}{(0.03)^2} = \frac{36 \times 10^{-3}}{9 \times 10^{-4}} = 40 \, \text{N} \) (Right)

Step 2: Resultant
\( \Sigma F_B = F_{BC} - F_{BA} = 40 - 20 = \mathbf{20 \, \text{N}} \) to the Right.

Problem 2 (Perpendicular Fields): Point P is located \(3 \, \text{cm}\) exactly above charge Q1 (\(+4 \, \mu\text{C}\)) and \(4 \, \text{cm}\) to the left of charge Q2 (\(-3 \, \mu\text{C}\)). If the constant \( k = 9 \times 10^9 \), calculate the total electric field strength at point P!

Step 1: E from each charge
\( E_1 = 9 \times 10^9 \frac{4 \times 10^{-6}}{(0.03)^2} = 4 \times 10^7 \, \text{N/C} \) (Upwards, away from Q1)

\( E_2 = 9 \times 10^9 \frac{3 \times 10^{-6}}{(0.04)^2} = \frac{27 \times 10^3}{16 \times 10^{-4}} = 1.6875 \times 10^7 \, \text{N/C} \) (Right, towards Q2)

Step 2: Pythagorean Resultant
\( E_P = \sqrt{(4 \times 10^7)^2 + (1.6875 \times 10^7)^2} \approx \mathbf{4.34 \times 10^7 \, \text{N/C}} \)

Problem 3 (Resultant Challenge): Two attractive electrical forces act on a test charge. The first force is \( 6 \, \text{N} \) pointing North, and the second force is \( 8 \, \text{N} \) pointing East. What is the magnitude of the resultant force experienced by the charge?

North and East form a \( 90^\circ \) angle. Use Pythagoras:

\[ R = \sqrt{F_{\text{North}}^2 + F_{\text{East}}^2} \] \[ R = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = \mathbf{10 \, \text{N}} \]

(Matches the 3-4-5 triangle pattern: \( 3(2) \)-div-\( 4(2) \)-div-\( 5(2) \))

Problem 4 (Midpoint Field): Point A is located exactly in the middle between charge Q1 (\(+2 \, \mu\text{C}\)) and charge Q2 (\(-4 \, \mu\text{C}\)), which are separated by a total distance of \(6 \, \text{cm}\). Calculate the total electric field strength and determine its direction at point A!

Step 1: Distance to A
\( r_1 = r_2 = 3 \, \text{cm} = 0.03 \, \text{m} \)

Step 2: Field Vectors
\( E_1 = 9 \times 10^9 \frac{2 \times 10^{-6}}{0.0009} = 2 \times 10^7 \, \text{N/C} \) (Right, away from +)
\( E_2 = 9 \times 10^9 \frac{4 \times 10^{-6}}{0.0009} = 4 \times 10^7 \, \text{N/C} \) (Right, towards −)

Step 3: Resultant
Both vectors point in the same direction, so we add them:
\( E_A = E_1 + E_2 = \mathbf{6 \times 10^7 \, \text{N/C}} \) to the Right (towards Q2).

Problem 5 (Perpendicular Forces): A test charge experiences a Coulomb force of \( 15 \, \text{N} \) to the West and \( 20 \, \text{N} \) to the South due to nearby charges. Determine the magnitude of the resultant force acting on the test charge!

West and South form a \( 90^\circ \) angle. Use Pythagoras:

\[ R = \sqrt{15^2 + 20^2} \] \[ R = \sqrt{225 + 400} \] \[ R = \sqrt{625} = \mathbf{25 \, \text{N}} \]

(Matches the 3-4-5 triangle pattern: \( 3(5) \)-div-\( 4(5) \)-div-\( 5(5) \))
